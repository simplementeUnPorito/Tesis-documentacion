\begin{abstract}

Programmable mixed-signal platforms provide an attractive foundation for low-cost scientific instrumentation, but the limited precision of their configurable analog resources can constrain front-end performance. This paper proposes a Digitally Assisted Analog Front-End (DA-AFE) that non-intrusively exploits embedded mixed-signal resources to improve the operating conditions of a configurable analog front end while preserving a conventional analog acquisition path. As a proof of feasibility, the approach is demonstrated through automatic foreground DC-offset calibration of a multistage geophone conditioning chain. Experimental evaluation on two independently assembled acquisition boards under eight board--gain configurations showed that, relative to a common fixed-reference baseline, calibration reduced the residual DC offset at the low-pass output from 2.9--45.8\% to 0.4--3.9\% of the nominal ADC input span using a single controller configuration across all reported conditions. These results demonstrate the feasibility of embedded digital assistance for improving the operating conditions of low-cost configurable analog front ends without modifying the acquisition signal path during normal operation.

\end{abstract}
